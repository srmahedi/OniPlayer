OniPlayer Controls Guide
====================

Keyboard Controls
---------------
Playback:
- Space: Play/Pause
- Enter: Toggle fullscreen
- F: Toggle fullscreen
- Left Arrow: Seek backward
- Right Arrow: Seek forward
- Up Arrow: Volume up
- Down Arrow: Volume down
- M: Toggle mute
- Esc: Exit full screen
- Page Up: Previous video
- Page Down: Next video
- A: Cycle to next audio track
- S: Toggle subtitles on/off

Mouse Controls
------------
Playback:
- Left button Double-click: Play/Pause
- Middle-click: Toggle full screen
- Hold middle-click (1 second): Toggle subtitles on/off
- Right-click: Show context menu
- Scroll wheel: Adjust volume
- Left click + scroll: Fast forward/rewind
- Right click + scroll: Change audio track (up/down)
- Click and drag timeline: Seek through video
- Hold left-button + Single-click right-button: Next video
- Hold right-button + Single-click left-button: Previous video

Additional Features
----------------
- Full screen mode is the default mode
- Mouse cursor will not appear on the video screen
- Cursor will appear when the context menu is open
- Cursor will appear if hover the cursor on the timeline or title bar

Context Menu (Right-Click)
------------------------
Playback:
- Play/Pause
- Next Video
- Previous Video
- Toggle Fullscreen
- Audio Selection
- Subtitle Selction
- Sync Subtitle

Audio:
- Select Audio Track
- Toggle Mute
- Volume Control

Subtitles:
- Select Subtitle Track
- Subtitle Sync Adjustment
- Disable Subtitles

Timeline Controls
---------------
- Click anywhere on timeline to jump to that position
- Drag timeline slider for precise seeking
- Hover over timeline to preview time position
- Current time and total duration displayed

Volume Controls
-------------
- Click volume icon to toggle mute
- Drag volume slider to adjust volume
- Volume level indicator appears when changing volume
- Last volume level is remembered when unmuting
- Use mouse scroll wheel to adjust volume
